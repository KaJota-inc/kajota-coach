## What a good answer looks like

- "Yes, we pay to any mainnet account the team provides; no KYC up to $X, KYC above $X" → GO confirmed.
- "Yes, no KYC, standard mainnet payout" → GO confirmed, best case.
- "We follow OFAC; Nigeria is not restricted" → GO confirmed.
- "Payouts are handled by [vendor] and we don't know their jurisdiction list" → ESCALATE to DM; this answer isn't a yes.
- Silence after 72h → ESCALATE to DM a moderator; do not assume yes.

## What a bad answer looks like

- Any mention of a jurisdiction list that includes Nigeria → **STOP build immediately**, raise with user.
- A link to a KYC vendor requiring documents the user cannot easily provide → raise with user before continuing.

## Why we're not asking by email or form

The hackathon has no eligibility email or form. The only public contact channels listed on nearhackathon.com are Discord and the NEAR Protocol X account. Discord is faster and surfaces the answer to other builders (which is also good signal — if nobody else is asking, we might be the only Nigeria-based team).
